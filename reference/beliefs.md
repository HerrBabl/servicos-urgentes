# beliefs.md — Serviços Urgentes

The operating principles behind editorial and strategic decisions. Useful for CC when a judgment call isn't explicitly covered by a rule elsewhere — reason from these.

## Core beliefs

**1. We are a connector, not an authority.**
Serviços Urgentes doesn't employ, certify, or guarantee professionals. Every piece of copy should be checkable against this: does this sentence imply we vouch for someone in a way we can't back up? If so, rewrite it. This is why "profissionais verificados" is fine (implies a listing standard) but "aprovado por nós" is not (implies an evaluative judgment call we don't formally make).

**2. Never invent data, ever — flag the gap instead.**
A plausible-sounding price or timeframe is worse than an honest gap, because it erodes trust the moment a reader's real experience doesn't match it, and because GEO/AI citation engines will surface it as fact. If a number isn't in the page's own sourced content, don't generate it — say so and ask.

**3. Specificity beats generic authority claims.**
"Atendimento rápido em SJC" is weak. "Atendimento rápido no Aquarius, Urbanova e Vila Adyana" is strong. Naming real neighborhoods, streets, and landmarks is both a GEO signal (AI engines cite specific, checkable claims) and a trust signal for a stressed reader trying to confirm we actually know their area.

**4. Individual framing beats corporate framing, and it's also just accurate.**
Ian operates this without a CNPJ. Rather than treat that as a weakness to paper over, the site and outreach lean into "eu mantenho um diretório" honesty. This has proven to outperform corporate-sounding language in cold outreach and carries no compliance exposure. Don't inflate the operation's size or formality in copy or emails.

**5. Directory language isn't a legal formality — it's the whole trust model.**
If the site sounds like it employs "our team of technicians," a reader who has a bad experience with a listed professional will (correctly) blame the directory. Keeping language third-person and connector-focused protects both the reader's expectations and the site's liability. This is why it's enforced sitewide with zero tolerance, not just a style preference.

**6. An emergency reader's time is the top priority in copy structure.**
Every structural rule (front-loaded answers, "Resumo rápido" callouts, FAQ answers that lead with the number) exists because someone reading this might be standing outside a locked door at midnight. Structure always serves speed-to-answer over completeness-for-completeness's-sake.

**7. Content depth is a GEO strategy, not just an SEO one.**
The site optimizes to be citable by AI answer engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT), not just to rank on Google. That means: structured comparison data, FAQ schema, specific verifiable claims, and provider-level detail (ratings, addresses) matter as much as keyword targeting. When in doubt between "what ranks" and "what's citable," favor citable — it's the differentiator against larger, better-funded competitors with more backlinks.

**8. Real backlinks come from real relevance, not schemes.**
Outreach targets should be sites where a Serviços Urgentes link is a genuine value-add to their existing content — not link exchanges, not reciprocal directory listings, not volume-based cold email blasts. This is why competing local directories (e.g., Guia SJC) and large marketplaces (Cronoshare) are deprioritized as outreach targets even when they rank — a real editorial fit with a small, active, relevant blog beats DR alone.

**9. Small, quality signals compound; big claims don't.**
No exaggerated review counts, no invented star ratings, no manufactured urgency beyond what's real. The site's credibility is built from a lot of small, honest, checkable details (exact street names, real arrival-time ranges sourced from actual page content, accurate FAQ answers) rather than one big trust claim.

## How to apply these when a rule doesn't explicitly cover a situation

Ask: does this choice make the directory sound bigger, more certain, or more authoritative than it actually is? If yes, scale it back. Does it help a stressed reader get to the right professional faster, with accurate information? If yes, prioritize it.
