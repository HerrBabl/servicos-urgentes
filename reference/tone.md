# tone.md — Serviços Urgentes

How the site should *sound* in pt-BR. This is about register and emotional temperature, not vocabulary choices (see vocabulary.md) or facts (see business-context.md).

## Core register: Urgency + empathy, direct, calming, authoritative

The reader is often mid-emergency: locked out, flooded, no power, no AC in a heat wave. They're stressed, sometimes at 2am, often on a phone with a cracked screen and 4G. The copy's job is to calm them down and get them to the next action fast.

- **Direct, not chatty.** Short sentences. Lead with the answer, not a windup.
- **Calming, not clinical.** Acknowledge the stress implicitly through pacing and reassurance, not through explicitly naming their emotions ("Sabemos que isso é estressante" is acceptable sparingly; don't overuse empathy language to the point it delays the actual help).
- **Authoritative, not corporate.** No jargon, no "soluções inovadoras," no marketing fluff. Write like someone who actually knows SJC and has seen this problem before.
- **Confident without overpromising.** We're a directory, not a guarantee. Never promise outcomes we don't control ("resolve na hora garantido" — no; "atendimento rápido, normalmente em minutos" — yes, if sourced from page data).

## What this looks like in practice

**Opening a page (Resumo rápido / lede):** State the problem, state that help exists, state roughly how fast. No throat-clearing.

**FAQ answers:** Answer first sentence, context after. A reader scanning in a panic should get the number/timeframe/answer in the first 10 words.

**Bairro pages:** Slightly warmer, more descriptive — these aren't emergency-mode pages, they're context pages. Still no corporate voice, but room for local color (streets, landmarks, character of the neighborhood).

**Blog/guia posts:** Can have more breathing room and structure (H2s, lists) since the reader isn't necessarily mid-crisis, but keep sentences short and skip preamble.

## Never

- Never sound like an ad. No exclamation-heavy hype copy.
- Never sound like a government pamphlet. No passive-voice bureaucratic phrasing.
- Never manufacture urgency that isn't real ("ÚLTIMA CHANCE," fake scarcity). The urgency is already real (a burst pipe); we don't need to invent more.
- Never use humor in emergency-scenario copy (chaveiro-24h, emergências pages). Humor is fine in lighter blog content (e.g., economy tips, prevention guides) but never on a page someone's reading during an active crisis.

## Authority language (approved phrases)

- "Técnicos Verificados"
- "Atendimento Imediato"
- "Profissionais com 4+ estrelas"
- "Profissionais da região" / "Prestadores locais" / "Técnicos cadastrados" (see content-rules.md for directory-language pairs)
