# vocabulary.md — Serviços Urgentes

Word- and phrase-level choices. Pairs with tone.md (register) and content-rules.md (hard rules).

## Directory-language substitutions (see content-rules.md for the full non-negotiable table)

Quick reference — always prefer the right column:
- Nossos parceiros → Profissionais da região
- Nossa equipe → Técnicos cadastrados
- Nossa rede → Prestadores locais
- Nosso time → Profissionais verificados

## Preferred terms

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| a partir de R$X | em R$X (for lower bounds) |
| atendimento imediato / atendimento 24h | resposta rápida garantida (overpromises) |
| profissionais verificados | profissionais aprovados (implies vetting we don't formally do) |
| chame um profissional | contrate um especialista (too sales-y for an emergency-redirect moment) |
| encanador, eletricista, chaveiro, desentupidora, marido de aluguel, ar-condicionado | anglicisms or overly formal alternatives (e.g. avoid "handyman" — always "marido de aluguel") |
| bairro / região | zona (unless the official name includes "zona," e.g. Zona Sul) |

## Modifiers for titles (rotate, don't overuse any one)

Guia, 2026, 24h, Emergência, Completo — per Title Rules in the main spec.

## Words/phrases to avoid entirely

- "Soluções inovadoras," "sinergia," "parceria estratégica" — corporate jargon, doesn't fit the register at all.
- "Garantido," "garantia de resultado" — avoid absolute guarantees about outcomes outside our control (timing, price, availability).
- "Aprovado por nós," "avaliado e aprovado" — implies an evaluative authority the directory doesn't formally claim (this was explicitly removed from badge-outreach email copy; same logic applies sitewide).
- "O diretório" when referring to Serviços Urgentes in first-person outreach contexts — prefer "um diretório" (see business-context.md, outreach section) to keep individual framing accurate given no-CNPJ status.

## Regional/local vocabulary (use naturally, don't force into every paragraph)

Real street references only — never invent one:
- Aquarius = Av. Cassiano Ricardo
- Urbanova = Av. Possidônio José de Freitas

Neighborhood names should appear as their proper local names (Jardim Aquarius, Urbanova, Vila Adyana, Jardim das Colinas, Jardim Satélite, Jardim Esplanada, Bosque dos Eucaliptos, Parque Residencial Aquarius, Parque Industrial, Centro, São Dimas, Santana) — not generic descriptors.

## Punctuation & formatting conventions

- FAQ questions end with "?" — natural spoken-language phrasing, not keyword-stuffed.
- Avoid em-dash overuse in body copy; short sentences over dash-linked long ones, consistent with the direct/urgent register.
